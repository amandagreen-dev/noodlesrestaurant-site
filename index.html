Noodles Restaurant is a casual neighborhood restaurant in North Madison. We cook noodle bowls, pasta, soups and salads to order, and most of the menu is available for takeout. We also prepare trays for small parties and office lunches. Email our orders desk with your request and we will reply during business hours.
